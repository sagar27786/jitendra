About

A voice shaped
by pure devotion

Jitendra Dhasmana is a devoted amateur vocalist from India, channeling a lifetime of love for Hindi music into heartfelt performances that have quietly touched hundreds of thousands of listeners around the world.

His channel is a tribute to the golden era of Bollywood — a sanctuary of nostalgia, joy, and honest artistry, entirely free from commercial ambition. Every recording is an act of passion, not performance.

Over 13 years and 110 videos, he has built something rare: a deeply personal musical archive, open to the world — and always open to feedback.

"I started this channel because of my passion for music — and in the search for suggestions to improve my singing."

— Jitendra Dhasmana
